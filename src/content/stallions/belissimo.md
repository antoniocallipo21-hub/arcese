Belissimo is the 2025 NRHA Level 4 Open Futurity Champion. Ridden by Casey Deary, he scored 231.5 in Oklahoma City, and has since added placings at the 100X Reining Classic and the NRBC Breeders Cup, bringing his lifetime earnings to $465,365.

By NRHA Hall of Famer and $13 million sire Magnum Chic Dream, he is out of Americasnextgunmodel, the Million Dollar Dam that carried Casey Deary to the 2012 NRHA Futurity title. Bred and raised by Arcese Quarter Horses, he stands at Oswood Stallion Station.
