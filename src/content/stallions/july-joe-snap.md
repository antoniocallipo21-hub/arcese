July Joe Snap is the epitome of modern reining: power, precision and an extraordinary winning mindset. By the legendary Master Snapper and out of San July Jo Magnetic, this chestnut stallion has dominated the European scene since his debut.

In 2023 he won the NRHA European Futurity Open Level 4 with a score of 232, and in 2024 he was champion at the IRHA Derby and the 100X European Classic. His athletic build and his ability to perform high-difficulty maneuvers make him one of the most sought-after stallions for international breeding.
