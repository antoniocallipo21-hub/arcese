Gunnatrashya is one of the most influential reining stallions in modern history. By the legendary Colonels Smoking Gun (Gunner) out of Natrasha, he dominated the show pen, winning the 2009 NRHA Open Futurity and the 2010 NRHA Open Derby.

Inducted into the NRHA Hall of Fame in 2023, he has gone on to rewrite the breeding record books as a true champion-maker.
