Walla Walla Whiz is a living legend of world reining. By the "Sire of Sires" Topsail Whiz and out of the extraordinary mare Shiney Walla Wanda (a daughter of Shining Spark), he made history by winning the 2007 NRHA Open Derby with Shawn Flarida in the saddle.

A chestnut stallion with exceptional competitive longevity and breeding capability, he is an NRHA Million Dollar Sire. His offspring are known for their work ethic, superior maneuverability and incredible stop, qualities that continue to dominate the most important show arenas worldwide.
